# Source Contract

## Confirmed product vision

The product name is **SCHOLARSHIP HUNTER** and the tagline is **“Find Your Opportunity. Fund Your Future.”** It is an AI-powered global scholarship discovery and application intelligence platform, not a static scholarship directory.

The product should understand an applicant’s study goals and field, desired countries, academic qualifications, financial-support need, existing qualifications, realistic eligibility, and improvements needed before applying. It should turn discovery and application work into a strategic, personalized, achievable journey: discover relevant opportunities, explain fit and eligibility, support preparation and application management, and handle deadlines.

The experience should feel sophisticated and premium, powerful without intimidation, intelligent but human, purposeful in every interaction, and intentional screen by screen. Avoid generic AI dashboards and generic education-directory UI.

## Limits of the source

This contract is based only on visible source text. It does not define a technology stack, architecture, API names, data schemas, integrations, modules, ranking formula, scholarship inventory, eligibility policy, design system, business model, or measurable business targets. The repo may change; inspect its actual current state before every implementation.

Do not present recommendations as confirmed requirements. Label proposals, assumptions, unknowns, and decisions needed separately. Resolve conflicts only from real, accessible user requirements, repository conventions, or authoritative source material; record unresolved conflicts rather than silently selecting an invented answer.

## Useful interpretation

Personalization means using applicant-provided goals and qualifications to explain relevance and gaps, not claiming guaranteed admission, funding, eligibility, or outcomes. “Realistic” requires evidence and uncertainty, not a promise. “Global” is intended product reach; it is not proof that any particular country or award is covered.
